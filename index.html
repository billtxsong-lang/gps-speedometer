Under the hood

From satellites to a needle

Two independent estimates — receiver-reported speed and displacement over time — are blended and smoothed into one readable number.

Displacement ÷ time

Haversine

Each new fix is compared with the previous one on a great-circle path. Distance divided by the timestamp gap gives a ground-speed estimate.

Receiver speed

Doppler

Most GPS chips also report speed directly from satellite Doppler shift. It is usually steadier than differencing two positions.

Smart smoothing

EMA

A short exponential moving average hides fix-to-fix jitter while still letting real acceleration show up within a second or two.

Field notes

Getting a clean reading

Clear sky

Keep the device where it can see open sky. Tunnels, parkades and tall buildings can block the fix or make the speed lag.

Give it a moment

The first fix after opening the page can take 10–30 seconds. Accuracy usually improves over the next few fixes.

Battery

High-accuracy GPS is power-hungry. Stop tracking when you are done, especially on a long trip with the screen on.

Questions

GPS speedometer FAQ

How does a GPS speedometer work?

Your device receives position fixes from satellites. The page compares successive positions and timestamps to estimate displacement over time, and also uses the speed value the GPS receiver reports directly. The result is smoothed so the needle does not jump on every noisy fix.

How accurate is GPS speed?

GPS speed is usually more accurate than GPS position: consumer receivers often report speed within about 0.5 to 2 km/h when the sky view is clear. Buildings, tunnels, tree cover and a cold start can reduce accuracy or cause gaps.

Does the GPS speedometer need mobile data?

No. Satellite positioning works without a data connection, but the page itself must be loaded once. Some devices use network assistance for a faster first fix, which can improve startup time.

Why does the speed jump or stay at zero?

A GPS fix has a few metres of uncertainty, so a stationary device can drift. The gauge ignores tiny movements and smooths sudden jumps. If the speed stays at zero, check that location permission is allowed and that you have a clear view of the sky.

Can I switch between km/h and mph?

Yes. The unit switch changes the gauge, top speed and average speed between kilometres per hour and miles per hour, and changes the secondary speed between metres per second and feet per second. Distance switches between metres/kilometres and feet/miles.
